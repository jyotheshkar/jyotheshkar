## Keele University

- Official website header asset: https://www.keele.ac.uk/k-core/media/header/logo.svg
- Local file: `assets/brands/keele-original.svg`
- The original transparent SVG paths and `#271e3d` colour are retained. This is the current university website's horizontal identity, including Est 1949.
- The artwork is displayed on a pale mount inside the dark education card to preserve contrast without recolouring the logo. The image itself remains transparent.
- Brand reference and official download: https://www.keele.ac.uk/brand/

## Amazon

- Official Press Center: https://press.aboutamazon.com/logos?p=4
- Official original-colour asset: https://assets.aboutamazon.com/2e/d7/ac71f1f344c39f8949f48fc89e71/amazon-logo-squid-ink-smile-orange.png
- Local file: `assets/brands/amazon-original.png`, downloaded unchanged from the official URL above.
- Uses the original squid-ink wordmark and orange smile, on a pale mount for contrast. The monochrome Simple Icons Amazon glyph is superseded for the career card.

These logos identify the institutions in the owner's experience and education; they do not imply endorsement.
